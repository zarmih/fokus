# Batch I

This batch adds 3 new unique cognitive exercises:

1. **lag-echo** (Эхо с задержкой)
   - Domain: Memory (working_memory, spatial_memory)
   - Metric: memory-span
   - Mechanics: A sequence of positions flashes on a grid. After a delay, the player must repeat the sequence in the exact order. The grid size and sequence length increase with the level, testing both spatial working memory and retention after a delay.

2. **zone-guard** (Страж зоны)
   - Domain: Attention (sustained_attention, selective_attention)
   - Metric: speed-accuracy
   - Mechanics: Objects drift towards a central "zone". The player must tap only on the "threat" objects (red) while ignoring the "safe" objects (green). Misses and false alarms result in penalties. The speed and spawn rate increase with the level.

3. **sign-flip** (Смена знака)
   - Domain: Flexibility (inhibition, task_switching)
   - Metric: speed-accuracy
   - Mechanics: The player is presented with a simple arithmetic problem (+ or -) and multiple-choice answers. Occasionally, after a brief delay, the operator sign will suddenly flip (e.g., from + to -). The player must inhibit their initial response and answer based on the currently visible sign.
