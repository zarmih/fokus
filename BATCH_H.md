# Batch H Exercises

This batch introduces 3 new unique cognitive exercises focusing on different domains:

## 1. Пропуск пульса (pulse-gap)
- **Domain:** Speed
- **Skills:** Reaction speed, processing speed
- **Metric:** Timing precision
- **Mechanics:** A visual metronome flashes a sequence of elements. One of the beats is skipped while maintaining the rhythm's delay. After the sequence is complete, the player must identify and select the element that was skipped. As the level increases, the tempo gets faster and more elements are added.

## 2. Смена установки (set-shift)
- **Domain:** Flexibility
- **Skills:** Task switching, cognitive flexibility
- **Metric:** Speed-accuracy
- **Mechanics:** Classic set-shifting task. The player classifies a central card into one of two bins based on a prominently displayed rule (ЦВЕТ or ФОРМА). Target cards are mixed (e.g., Red Circle or Blue Square) so that classifying by color leads to a different bin than classifying by shape. The rule stays constant for a few rounds and then suddenly switches, testing cognitive flexibility.

## 3. Толпа (crowd-probe)
- **Domain:** Attention
- **Skills:** Visual scanning, selective attention
- **Metric:** Speed-accuracy
- **Mechanics:** A crowd of shapes is briefly flashed on the screen. The player is asked to identify how many targets of a specific color and shape (e.g., Red Circles) were present among the distractors. After the flash, the player selects a number from 1 to 5. Higher levels increase the number of distractors and reduce the exposure time.
